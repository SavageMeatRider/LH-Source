How it works

Engineered for the
last second.

Three systems working in sync to make sure you're never the one holding the tag.

01 / SCAN
📡
Scoreboard Sync
Reads the TNT Tag countdown from the scoreboard every millisecond. The moment a round starts, the timer is locked in with nanosecond precision.
02 / MEASURE
🔬
Live Ping Sampling
Samples your network latency continuously using a rolling 30-sample window and P95 outlier detection. Your fire window adapts to your actual connection — not a guess.
03 / FIRE
⚡
Nanosecond Fire Thread
A dedicated high-priority thread checks every nanosecond. When the timer hits the calculated fire window, it attacks instantly — far faster than any human reaction can.
